# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: readers in India who come to chintan.news from a shared story link, a Play Store listing or word of mouth, deciding whether Chintan is worth installing. Closed-test testers also land here for feedback.

Secondary surface with a different user: the Chintan Desk at /admin, a private tool used only by the founder to add stories to the app by hand. It is product register, not brand: design there serves the task.

## Product Purpose

chintan.news explains Chintan, the news app for people who read to understand, and gets visitors into the app. It also hosts the pages the app depends on: shared-story landing pages, privacy and data-safety pages, account deletion, tester feedback, and the Desk.

The Desk exists so the founder keeps editorial control over what the app shows: add a story the news API missed or hasn't delivered yet, mark how big it is, and track developing stories. Success is publishing an accurate, sourced story from a phone in a couple of minutes, without second-guessing whether it worked.

## Positioning

The news app for people who read to understand: fewer, better stories that update instead of repeat. "Don't just consume. Contemplate."

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: get the app (Google Play, App Store when live).
- Secondary: read a shared story on the web first; tester feedback for people already in the test.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Don't just consume. Contemplate.
- Belief ladder: the infinite feed is the problem → Chintan is built differently (three briefs a day, stories that update, the other side of an argument) → it is worth installing.
- Proof on hand: none collected yet.

## Brand Personality

Contemplative, editorial, unhurried. Serif headlines and quiet dark surfaces, with Surya red used sparingly as the one point of heat.

For the Desk specifically: a calm newsroom desk. Editorial and unhurried even when news is breaking; clear, confident, one thing at a time. It should feel like the Chintan app, not like an admin panel.

## Anti-references

For the Desk: WordPress and other CMS editors (toolbars, meta boxes, settings everywhere), and security-theatre login pages (padlocks, shields, "secure portal" banners, alarming red warnings). Security there is real and quiet, never decorative.

## Design Principles

1. One thing at a time. Each screen asks for one decision; the next step appears when it's needed, not before.
2. Calm under pressure. Breaking news is when the tool gets used, so it must stay legible and steady then, not flash and shout.
3. Honest state. Always say plainly what is happening (researching, ready, published, closing in 6h) and what went wrong in words that say what to do next.
4. Same family as the app. Chintan's type, colour and voice carry through; nothing looks borrowed from a template kit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The Desk is used mostly on a phone, often one-handed: large touch targets, primary actions within thumb reach, readable in daylight, reduced motion respected.
